# Enterprise Evaluation & Licensing

**Classification:** `PUBLIC · NON-CONFIDENTIAL · COMMERCIAL ENTRY POINT`  
**Organization:** AETHER X GLOBAL

---

## Purpose

AETHER X GLOBAL supports qualified organizations that want to evaluate Governed Intelligence for enterprise, institutional or strategic use.

The evaluation process is designed to provide sufficient technical evidence for a serious diligence decision while preserving AETHER X intellectual property, confidential implementation detail and trade-secret material.

---

## Standard Engagement Path

### Stage 1 — Non-confidential discussion

Objective:

- understand the organization's problem;
- establish fit with the Governed Intelligence problem class;
- identify the technical and commercial diligence questions;
- avoid unnecessary disclosure before scope is known.

No proprietary source code or restricted implementation material is provided at this stage.

### Stage 2 — NDA / evaluation terms

Where deeper disclosure is justified, the parties establish an appropriate confidentiality and evaluation framework.

Terms may address, as appropriate:

- permitted evaluation purpose;
- confidentiality;
- access control;
- evaluation duration;
- authorized personnel;
- restrictions on redistribution and sublicensing;
- ownership of pre-existing intellectual property;
- handling of evaluation artifacts;
- return, deletion or expiry obligations;
- publication and publicity boundaries.

Final legal terms are transaction-specific.

### Stage 3 — Controlled technical diligence

AETHER X may provide a bounded package appropriate to the agreed purpose, potentially including:

- technical briefing;
- architecture review;
- selected engineering-assurance evidence;
- bounded demonstration;
- exact artifact identity;
- controlled interface documentation;
- risk and limitation discussion.

Disclosure is purpose-limited and need-to-know.

### Stage 4 — Bounded evaluation / pilot

The preferred technical model is **hosted or controlled evaluation**, so that proprietary implementation remains under AETHER X control.

Where appropriate, an evaluation may be identified using a customer-specific reference such as:

```text
AX-EVAL-[CUSTOMER]-[UNIQUE-ID]
```

An evaluation record may include:

- customer / evaluator identity;
- approved purpose;
- issue date;
- expiry date;
- artifact or service version;
- cryptographic digest where applicable;
- access scope;
- evaluation restrictions;
- AETHER X attribution.

### Stage 5 — Commercial agreement

If evaluation establishes value, the parties may consider a commercial structure such as:

- enterprise licence;
- hosted technology licence;
- integration and implementation agreement;
- strategic partnership;
- sector-specific licence;
- limited exclusivity;
- support and assurance services.

AETHER X's default commercial objective is to **retain ownership of the underlying technology** while licensing defined usage rights.

---

## Source-Code Position

Source-code delivery is **not** part of the standard evaluation path.

A request for source access is treated as a materially different transaction because it creates substantially different intellectual-property, security, support and leakage risk.

Where source access is ever considered, it requires explicit commercial and legal authorization beyond ordinary technical evaluation.

---

## Preferred Commercial Principle

```text
TECHNOLOGY OWNERSHIP: AETHER X GLOBAL
        ↓
CUSTOMER RECEIVES: DEFINED USAGE RIGHTS
        ↓
SCOPE: PURPOSE / FIELD / TERM / TERRITORY / ACCESS MODEL AS AGREED
```

Commercial rights should be no broader than necessary for the customer objective.

Any exclusivity should be explicitly scoped and commercially priced for the rights being withheld from other markets or customers.

---

## Evaluation Safety

AETHER X may use technical and administrative controls including:

- hosted execution;
- limited interfaces;
- customer-specific evaluation identifiers;
- signed or hashed artifacts;
- expiry controls;
- access logs;
- purpose-limited credentials;
- controlled data rooms;
- disclosure registers;
- customer-specific manifests.

No single mechanism is treated as sufficient by itself. Technical controls and contractual controls are designed to reinforce each other.

---

## What an Evaluation Does Not Grant

Unless expressly stated in a signed agreement, evaluation access does not grant:

- ownership of AETHER X technology;
- unrestricted production rights;
- redistribution rights;
- sublicensing rights;
- right to publish confidential material;
- right to represent AETHER X technology as the evaluator's technology;
- source-code rights;
- patent rights;
- trademark rights;
- exclusivity.
